# All-root admission ownership prerequisite

This bounded T026 prerequisite implements the lock-order requirement in the [multiroot contract](../contracts/multiroot-protection.md). It does not accept T026 or establish durable multiroot state protection, Loading ownership, offline snapshots or deletion readiness.

Actual multi-path admission acquires every distinct physical root in canonical order before any member lock. With every root retained, it rereads each expected Complete ledger and derives the full root-local member-lock obligations. It then acquires those obligations in state-slot/root order, retaining separate physical locks for equal slots in different roots. All ledgers, native identities and profiles are replayed before member locators and payloads. One-root and bootstrap admission retain their existing paths.

Root-specific owners share one reference-counted native group. Each root's counted operation borrows drain before its share is released; every root/member lock and control-directory handle remains held until the final share drains. Partial acquisition, locator replay and owner-construction failures unwind retained resources in reverse, preserving cleanup errors. Positively Unenrolled paths remain Unenrolled and do not gain an enrolled borrow or expand path restrictions. Admission, physical locks and registry requests reuse one root comparer; test-side ordering expectations are independent.
